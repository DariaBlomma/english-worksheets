Lesson Plan

  1. Natural numbers and digits — what they are and how they differ.
  2. Place value — how the position of a digit changes its value.
  3. Periods — how large numbers are grouped into thousands, millions, and so on.
  4. Three forms of writing numbers — standard, word, and expanded form.

Part 1 · Natural Numbers and Digits

What natural numbers are. We use natural numbers to count objects. They start with 1 and go on forever.

How a number differs from a digit. There are only 10 digits: from 0 to 9. We use these digits to make any number, just like letters make words.

Key phrase: What is the difference between…?
Task 01
Explain the topic

Imagine you are teaching a 5th-grade math class. Explain the concept of natural numbers and digits to your students. Use the vocabulary from the Word Bank at the top of the page. Keep your sentences short, clear, and use the Present Simple tense.

Task 02
Check understanding

Formulate and ask 3 to 5 clarifying questions to your students to check if they understood the material. Use the Present Simple tense and the verb to be. Focus your questions on the properties of natural numbers and the difference between a number and a digit.
