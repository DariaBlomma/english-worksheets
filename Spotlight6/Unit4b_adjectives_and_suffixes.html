Adjective Suffixes: The "Root" Method

How to Build Adjectives

Every adjective has a "hidden root" inside it. Find the root — and you'll understand the meaning!

-IVE (from a VERB)
"Having the quality of doing this action"
Attract (притягивать) ➔ attractive (привлекательный)
Create (создавать) ➔ creative (творческий)
Act (действовать) ➔ active (активный)
-ABLE / -IBLE (from a VERB)
"Can be... / possible to..."
Enjoy (наслаждаться) ➔ enjoyable (приятный)
Comfort (комфорт) ➔ comfortable (удобный)
Eat (есть) ➔ edible (съедобный)
-IC (from a NOUN)
"Related to / typical of something"
Hero (герой) ➔ heroic (героический)
Fantasy (фантазия) ➔ fantastic (фантастический)
Drama (драма) ➔ dramatic (драматичный)
-ING (from a VERB)
"Causing this feeling" (about things)
Bore (наводить скуку) ➔ boring (скучный)
Excite (волновать) ➔ exciting (захватывающий)
Disgust (вызывать отвращение) ➔ disgusting (отвратительный)
-FUL vs -LESS (Opposites!)
-ful = "full of" | -less = "without"
Care (забота) ➔ careful (осторожный) vs careless (небрежный)
Use (польза) ➔ useful (полезный) vs useless (бесполезный)
1 Word Formation (ЕГЭ Format)

Read the text. Transform the words in capitals at the end of each line so that they fit grammatically and lexically. Use adjective suffixes!

My favourite TV show is a talent show. It is very and I watch it every week. (ENJOY)
The host of the show is a really person — he always makes people laugh. (CREATE)
The performances are — you never know what will happen next! (EXCITE)
Some reality shows, however, are completely . I never watch them. (USE)
Documentaries are usually very because you learn a lot of new things. (INTEREST)
My little brother loves cartoons, but I think they are a bit for my age. (CHILD)
The winner of the competition was truly — everyone stood up and clapped. (IMPRESS)
