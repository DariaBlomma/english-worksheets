Present Simple: Asking Questions

The Golden Rules

• Wh- Questions: Question Word + do/does + subject + verb (infinitive)? What time does the bus leave?
• Yes/No Questions: Do/Does + subject + verb (infinitive)? Does your father work here?
• Tag Questions: Positive statement + , don't/doesn't + subject? She drives carefully, doesn't she?
⚠️ Subject Questions (Who/What): Question Word + verb (-s) + ...? No do/does needed! Example: Who checks the tickets?
