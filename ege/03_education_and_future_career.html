Vocabulary

Education & School

Word / Phrase Transcription Translation Example
Higher education /ˌhaɪər ˌedʒuˈkeɪʃn/ высшее образование Many teenagers believe that higher education is essential for a successful future.
Core subjects /kɔːr ˈsʌbdʒekts/ основные предметы Maths and English are considered core subjects in most schools.
Extracurricular activities /ˌekstrəkəˈrɪkjələ(r) ækˈtɪvətiz/ внеклассные занятия Taking part in extracurricular activities helps students develop new skills.
To acquire knowledge / skills /əˈkwaɪər ˈnɒlɪdʒ/ приобретать знания / навыки School is not just about memorizing facts; it is about acquiring practical skills.
To cope with academic pressure /kəʊp wɪð ˌækəˈdemɪk ˈpreʃə(r)/ справляться с академической нагрузкой It is important for teenagers to know how to cope with academic pressure.

Future Career

Word / Phrase Transcription Translation Example
Career prospects /kəˈrɪə(r) ˈprɒspekts/ карьерные перспективы IT specialists usually have excellent career prospects in the modern world.
To pursue a career in... /pəˈsjuː ə kəˈrɪə(r) ɪn/ строить карьеру в... After school, I would like to pursue a career in medicine.
A well-paid job /ə wel peɪd dʒɒb/ высокооплачиваемая работа Many people choose their profession based on the desire to get a well-paid job.
To be in high demand /bi ɪn haɪ dɪˈmɑːnd/ быть очень востребованным Currently, engineers and programmers are in high demand all over the world.
Job satisfaction /dʒɒb ˌsætɪsˈfækʃn/ удовлетворенность работой For me, job satisfaction is more important than a high salary.

Development & Choice

Word / Phrase Transcription Translation Example

Lifelong learning /ˈlaɪflɒŋ ˈlɜːnɪŋ/ непрерывное обучение In today’s fast-changing world, lifelong learning is the key to staying competitive.

Exam Questions Practice

Speaking Task 3 (Interview)

Objective: Give full answers (2–3 sentences) to each question.

  • 1. What is your favourite school subject, and why do you like it?
  • 2. What extracurricular activities are popular in your school?
  • 3. How do you usually prepare for school exams?
  • 4. How do you cope with academic pressure before an important test?
  • 5. What profession would you like to pursue a career in, and why?
  • 6. Do you think it is necessary to get higher education to be successful nowadays?
  • 7. What skills do you think are in high demand in the modern job market?
  • 8. Is job satisfaction or a high salary more important to you? Explain your choice.

Speaking Task 4 (Monologue with photos)

Objective: Describe, compare, evaluate advantages/disadvantages, and express opinion.

  • Topic A: Ways of studying (Classroom vs. Online)
    Focus: developing soft skills, academic pressure, acquiring practical skills, stepping stone to future goals.
  • Topic B: Choosing a future profession (Manual labour vs. Office work)
    Focus: job satisfaction, career prospects, well-paid job, being in high demand, lifelong learning.

Writing Task 37 (Email)

Objective: Formulate 3 accurate questions to your pen-friend based on the prompt.

  • Prompt: School life
    ...What extracurricular activities do you enjoy the most? How many lessons do you usually have a day? What is your favourite core subject?
  • Prompt: Future plans
    ...Are you going to enter a university to get higher education? What profession are you planning to pursue a career in? Why did you choose this sphere?
  • Prompt: Part-time jobs
    ...Have you ever had a part-time job? Do you think it is easy for teenagers to find a well-paid job? What skills can teenagers acquire from working?
