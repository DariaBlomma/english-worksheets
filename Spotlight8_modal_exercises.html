📝 MODAL VERBS: PRACTICE

8th Grade · Exercises 1-4
🔵 Exercise 1 · Choose the Correct Modal
Circle the right option. Pay attention to the paraphrase in brackets!
1. You mustn't / don't have to use your phone during the exam. (it's forbidden)
2. You mustn't / don't have to wear a uniform tomorrow. (it isn't necessary)
3. You should / must revise more if you want to pass. (it's a good idea)
4. You should / must stop when the traffic light is red. (it's the law)
5. She can't / mustn't be at home. Her car isn't in the driveway. (deduction)
6. You needn't / mustn't bring food. We have plenty. (it isn't necessary)
7. Students can / may borrow books from the library. (permission)
8. He should / must see a doctor. His headache is terrible. (strong advice)

🔵 Exercise 4 · MUST vs HAVE TO
Fill in must, mustn't, have to, or don't have to. Use the context clue in brackets!
1. I call my mom tonight. (I feel it's important — internal obligation)
2. Students wear uniforms at this school. (it's the school rule — external obligation)
3. You smoke inside the hospital. (it's strictly forbidden — negative of must)
4. You come early tomorrow. It's a day off. (it's not necessary — negative of have to)
5. I finish this report today. (my boss told me to — external pressure)
🟣 Exercise 5 · CAN vs BE ABLE TO
Fill in the correct form: can / could / be able to. Pay attention to the tense and type of ability!
1. I swim when I was five years old. (general ability in the past)
2. After months of training, I finally run 10 km. (specific achievement in the past)
3. I help you with the project tomorrow. (future ability — "can" has no future form!)
4. She speak three languages fluently. (general present ability)
5. Despite the noise, I to finish the exam on time. (specific past achievement — note the "to"!)
🟡 Exercise 6 · MAY vs MIGHT vs COULD
Choose the best modal verb. Think: is it a real chance, a doubt, or just an option?
1. Take an umbrella. It rain later. (real possibility ~50% — weather forecast)
2. I come to the party, but I'm really tired. (small chance ~20-30% — don't count on it)
3. We order pizza, or we cook pasta. (discussing options, not chances)
4. Hurry up! The bus leave any minute. (real, factual possibility)
5. I stay home tonight. I've had a long day. (doubtful, remote possibility — I'm leaning towards staying)
🟢 Exercise 7 · Speaking Practice: Real-Life Scenarios
Read the situation. What would you say using a modal verb? Work in pairs!
🚫 Rules & Prohibition
You are in the school chemistry lab. There are dangerous chemicals on the tables. Your friend takes out a sandwich to eat.
Target: mustn't
💡 Giving Advice
Your friend has a terrible toothache but is very afraid of going to the dentist.
Target: should
🔍 Deduction (Present)
You see your neighbor carrying three huge, heavy grocery bags. He is sweating and walking very slowly.
Target: must be
🕵️ Deduction (Past)
Your classmate got 100% on the hardest math test, but you know he was playing video games all week and never opened the book.
TTarget: must have / might have
⏪ Past Unnecessary Action
You brought a huge, heavy umbrella to school this morning, but the sun has been shining brightly all day.
Target: needn't have + V3
😔 Past Regret
You stayed up until 3 AM watching TikTok videos, and now you can't keep your eyes open during the first lesson.
Target: shouldn't have + V3
